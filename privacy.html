Privacy Policy

E-KaagazBill by AAM Labs · Last updated: 23 November 2026

WhatsApp messages

E-KaagazBill (by AAM Labs) processes WhatsApp messages only to generate invoices. We read the messages, voice notes, or photos you send to E-KaagazBill solely to create the bill you asked for and to deliver it.

What we keep

E-KaagazBill does not store message content beyond what is needed to create and deliver the invoice. We keep the invoice itself (for example customer name, items, amounts, and GST details) so you can view, resend, and export your bills.

Payments

Payment data goes through Razorpay. E-KaagazBill does not store card, bank, or UPI credentials. Razorpay processes payments under its own privacy policy and security standards; we only receive the payment status needed to mark a bill as paid.

For any privacy question or request, email mangale.arihant@gmail.com.
